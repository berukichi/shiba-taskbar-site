Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Taskbar Shiba ("the app") does not collect or send any personal information about you.
(日本語版)

What the app uses

  • Your dog's data (name, coat, friendship level, personality and so on) is stored on your PC only. It is never sent anywhere.
  • How the PC is used: to shape your dog's personality, the app checks on your PC only how long it has been since you last used it and whether music is playing. It never records or sends what you type, what you listen to, or what is on your screen.
  • Sound level: so the dog can dance to music, the app reads the playback level (a single number from 0 to 1, the same one the Windows volume mixer shows). It never records, stores or sends any sound, and it does not use the microphone.
  • Weather: only if you pick an area in Settings, the app gets the weather from Open-Meteo (https://open-meteo.com/) about every 30 minutes. All it sends is the latitude and longitude of the area you picked; it does not use your PC's location. When you search for a town, the name you type is sent to Open-Meteo's place search. If you don't pick an area, the app never connects to the internet. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0).
  • Camera and microphone in use: so the dog stays quiet in calls, the app checks only whether the camera or microphone is being used right now, from Windows' own records (you can turn this off). It never reads any picture or sound.
  • Processor, battery and the front window: to change the dog's mood the app checks, on your PC only, how busy the processor is, the battery level, and the position and size of the window in front. It never reads what is in that window.
  • Photos and GIFs: pictures you take with "Take a photo" or "Record a GIF" are saved to Pictures › TaskbarShiba. They are drawn from the dog's own pixels, not captured from the screen.

Your dog's data is kept in the place Windows sets aside for each app, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

Outings

An outing card's code holds your dog's name, coat and friendship level, the date it was made and how long it is valid, and — only if you choose — its personality and your nickname. You decide who gets the code. The app never sends the code anywhere itself.

Codes and card pictures you receive are read on your PC only, and pictures are not kept. The name, owner nickname, coat and date of each dog that visits are kept on your PC as the guestbook. The codes you make are also kept on your PC, so the app can tell when a code is your own dog's.
